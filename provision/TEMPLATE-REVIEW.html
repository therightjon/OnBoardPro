Faculty Hire Templates — Strawman for HR Review

Generated 2026-08-11 from the live SharePoint lists (Templates, TemplateStages, TemplateTasks on the OBGYN Onboarding site) — this is what is actually seeded, not a plan.

Sources: Faculty Pre-Hire Process – Jon Steen.docx (refined) + Faculty Onboarding Checklist_May 2025.docx. Both templates are Draft — nothing expands onto a real candidate until activated.

How to read due rules: dates compute from anchors — LOI (letter of intent executed), LOO Issued / LOO Accepted (offer), Start (start date). "120 days before Start" becomes a real date per candidate; tasks whose anchor date isn't known yet wait as "Pending date" and fill in automatically.

Marking this up: strike tasks that are wrong, add missing ones, correct owners/timing — any format is fine.
